Tax

Standard 2026/27 allowance is £12,570. Adjust for Marriage Allowance transfer (±£1,260), Blind Person's Allowance (+£3,250), or a tapered allowance if total income exceeds £100,000.

Pension (salary sacrifice)

Statutory redundancy pay and any excess over £30,000 are not normally sacrificed. Sacrifice reduces both taxable and NIable pay on the notice/holiday pay elements, and reduces employer NI accordingly.

Student loan

Deducted at 9% (6% for Postgraduate) of pay above the monthly threshold, on the same pay this applies to as employee NI (notice + holiday pay; redundancy pay is exempt).

This tool provides estimates based on 2026/27 UK statutory rates (England, Wales & Northern Ireland; excludes Scottish income tax bands) and standard PAYE/NI mechanics, including pension salary sacrifice and student loan repayment (Plans 1, 2, 4, 5 and Postgraduate). It is not a substitute for advice from your employer's payroll team, an accountant, or HMRC. Multiple simultaneous student loan plans, tapered annual allowance pension charges, and salary sacrifice effects on employer contributions are not modelled.
