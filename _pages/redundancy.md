Being made redundant means a lot of numbers land on you at once: statutory redundancy pay, notice pay, holiday pay, and a payslip that's been taxed in a way that rarely matches what you actually owe for the year. This calculator brings those together for the 2026/27 tax year (England, Wales and Northern Ireland — Scottish income tax bands are not covered).

It works out your **age-banded statutory redundancy pay** year by year, adds **notice pay** (statutory minimum or contractual, whichever is longer), and calculates **pro-rata holiday pay** — including an option to carry forward untaken days from a previous leave year, relevant if long-term sickness has kept you from taking leave.

On the tax side, it applies the **£30,000 tax-free exemption** to redundancy pay, estimates **cumulative PAYE Income Tax** and **employee/employer National Insurance** on the taxable elements, and factors in **pension salary sacrifice** and **student loan repayments** (Plans 1, 2, 4, 5 and Postgraduate) where they apply. Because a large one-off payment is often over-taxed under cumulative PAYE, it also estimates your **true annual tax liability** and flags a likely refund if you have no further income this tax year.
