Enter your savings, premium bonds, cash and stocks and shares ISA values, and nominal growth/interest rate to work out how long it would last.

## Notes

> ignores inflation and future changes in purchasing power — all figures are today's £ money. Premium Bond "interest" is your chosen average prize rate and assume average luck, not a guaranteed return. The Stocks &amp; Shares ISA uses a single constant nominal return, which real markets never deliver smoothly — treat it as a long-run average, not a forecast. Assumes constant rates, constant monthly expense, and a constant marginal tax rate on the savings pot. Not financial advice obviously.
